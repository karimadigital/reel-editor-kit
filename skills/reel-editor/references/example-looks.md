# Example looks

Four finished looks from a real talking-head channel, kept as models for filling in `my-look.md`. They are
examples, not defaults: never apply one unless the user picks it.

## Shared rules these looks follow

- One display font, one caption font, and a serif only for intentional emotional emphasis.
- One-word-at-a-time captions. A restrained outline or shadow only when the footage needs contrast.
- Text stays inside the Reels safe zone and off the eyes, mouth and main hand gesture.
- Behind-head text only when the cutout is clean and at least 70% of every letter stays readable. If it looks
  pasted on, move it in front.
- Natural skin. Fix exposure and white balance before any creative grade. No film grain, orange skin or crushed
  blacks.
- Titles start on the first useful frame and hold long enough to read.

## Clean (everyday default)

- Display: Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold, mixed case.
- Captions: Inter Tight Black, one spoken word at a time, below the face and above the platform buttons.
- Color: white or cream with a restrained dark shadow; one butter-yellow word (`#FAE67A`) only when it carries
  the meaning.
- Motion: small scale and blur settle on the active word; no constant bouncing.
- Grade: neutral, lightly brightened, skin-safe.
- Use for: teaching, confessions, process explanations, anything that should still feel raw.

## Bold

- Display: Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold. Captions: Inter Tight Black.
- Color: white plus butter yellow; turquoise as a deliberate A/B swap.
- Motion: Tenfold's kinetic timing and punch-ins, but less crowded than the upstream Bold preset.
- Behind-head text: one major phrase per section at most.
- Use for: hooks, proof statements, numbered steps, strong opinions.

## Editorial

- Display: Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold, with DM Serif Display or Instrument Serif Italic for one emotional phrase.
- Color: cream plus one accent (butter yellow, soft pink `#F1A9D5`, or turquoise).
- Motion: slow masked serif reveal and restrained push-ins; no decorative timestamps or orbiting reels unless the
  script needs them.
- Grade: warm but neutral; room natural, subject lifted.
- Use for: founder stories, lifestyle footage, reflective lessons, named series.

## Series opener

- Display: large Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold in one signature color. Support: small white Inter Tight.
- Layout: centered and simple; no frame or logo.
- Use for: recurring series openers, not routine captions.

## Testing looks fairly

When comparing, keep the edit, grade, timing and placement the same and change one thing: the display font, the
accent color, or front-versus-behind-head text. Note which variable was tested. One reel never crowns a winner.
